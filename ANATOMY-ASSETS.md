# Biryani anatomy: component images needed

The scroll "anatomy" effect needs each part of the dish as its own transparent image.
Free stock photos do not come separated, and automatic cut-outs look rough, so generate
these with an AI image tool (Midjourney, Firefly, ChatGPT images, Canva) or photograph them.

Rules for every image:
- One single object, centred, pure black background (we remove it), no table, no text, no shadow under it.
- Same lighting for all: warm key light from top-left, soft rim light, macro studio food photograph, ultra realistic, 4k.
- Square, at least 1500 px. Save as PNG (or WebP) into `public/anatomy/<name>.png`.

| File | Prompt |
|---|---|
| `handi.png` | An empty traditional copper handi cooking pot with a brass rim and two small handles, three-quarter view from slightly above, open top showing the empty dark inside |
| `handi-lid.png` | A copper handi lid with a small round knob, three-quarter view |
| `dough-seal.png` | A rope of baked wheat dough forming a ring (the dum seal), top view |
| `rice-mound.png` | A heaped mound of cooked saffron basmati rice, long separate grains, yellow and white streaks, no plate, no bowl |
| `chicken-leg.png` | One bone-in chicken leg piece coated in glossy red-orange biryani masala, charred edges |
| `chicken-piece.png` | One boneless chicken tikka chunk, red-orange marinade, light char marks |
| `paneer-cube.png` | One cube of grilled paneer, creamy white with a few char marks and orange marinade |
| `fried-onions.png` | A small heap of crispy golden-brown fried onion strands (birista) |
| `potato.png` | One half of a golden fried potato (aloo) for the biryani |
| `egg.png` | One boiled egg cut in half, yolk visible |
| `mint-leaves.png` | A small sprig of fresh mint leaves |
| `coriander.png` | A small sprig of fresh coriander leaves |
| `green-chili.png` | Two fresh green chillies |
| `lemon-wedge.png` | One lemon wedge |
| `saffron.png` | A pinch of saffron threads, deep red |
| `whole-spices.png` | Green cardamom, cloves, a cinnamon stick, star anise and bay leaf in a small loose pile |
| `rice-grain.png` | One single cooked basmati rice grain, long and slightly curved (used hundreds of times by the animation) |

Optional extras (nice to have): `raita-bowl.png`, `salan-bowl.png`.
